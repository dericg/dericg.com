About me

I’m curious about how things work — and how they could work better.

I’m Deric Garza, a software engineer and systems builder. I’m at my best when things make sense—when the important information is easy to find, the process is clear, and people understand what’s happening.

A lot of my work comes from building the systems that make that possible.

I likeLearning unfamiliar systems quickly
I noticePatterns hiding inside messy information
I buildUseful tools, not technology for its own sake
I care aboutMaking complicated things easier for people
The projects on this site span different subjects because the subject changes. The kind of problem I enjoy solving usually doesn’t.
